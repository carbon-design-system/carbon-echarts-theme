## Usage

Histograms display the frequency distribution of a single continuous variable by dividing the data range into equal-width bins. They differ from bar charts in that the bars are gapless and the x-axis is continuous.

**When to use:**

- Understanding the distribution shape (normal, skewed, bimodal)
- Showing frequency or density of a continuous variable
- Exploring data before analysis

**When not to use:**

- Categorical data (use a bar chart)
- Comparing distributions across groups (use boxplots or density plots)

## Color

Use a **single categorical color** — histograms represent one variable.

## ECharts implementation notes

Implemented as a `bar` series with `barCategoryGap: '1%'` (near-zero gap). `createHistogramOptions` bins raw observation arrays automatically using Sturges' rule.
