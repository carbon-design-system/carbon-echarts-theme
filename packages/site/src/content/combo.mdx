title: Combo chart
---

## Usage

Combo charts combine two or more series types (typically bar and line) in the same chart area. Use them when comparing a discrete measure (bars) against a continuous trend (line) over the same categories.

**When to use:**
- Comparing actual vs. target across categories
- Overlaying a trend line on a bar chart
- Dual-axis when series have different value scales

**When not to use:**
- More than two series types (becomes confusing)
- When both series have similar scales (use a grouped bar chart instead)

## Color

Assign the first categorical color to bars and the second to lines. For dual-axis combos, associate each axis color with its series.

## ECharts implementation notes

Combo charts mix `bar` and `line` series in the same `series` array. Dual-axis uses `series[].yAxisIndex: 0` and `series[].yAxisIndex: 1` with two `yAxis` definitions.
