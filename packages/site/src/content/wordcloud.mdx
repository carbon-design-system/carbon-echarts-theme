## Usage

Word clouds (tag clouds) display text data where word size encodes frequency or weight. They provide a quick visual summary of text content and are common in content analysis, social media, and survey response visualization.

**When to use:**
- Showing relative frequency of terms in a text corpus
- Survey open-text response summaries
- Tag/keyword prominence at a glance

**When not to use:**
- Precise value comparison (word size perception is imprecise)
- Long phrases (only single words or short phrases work well)
- When exact counts matter (use a horizontal bar chart of terms)

## Color

Use the categorical palette. Assign colors randomly or by semantic category.
