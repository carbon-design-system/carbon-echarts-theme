title: Area chart
---

## Usage

Area charts are line charts with the area below the line filled in. Use them to show cumulative totals or stacked comparisons over time where the filled area communicates volume.

**When to use:**
- Showing volume or magnitude over time
- Displaying stacked totals where part-to-whole relationships matter
- Emphasizing the area between two series

**When not to use:**
- Precise value comparison (filled areas obscure differences — use a line chart)
- More than 4–5 stacked series (becomes unreadable)

## Variants

- **Simple** — single filled area below a line.
- **Stacked** — multiple series stacked on top of each other; shows total and individual contribution.

## Color

Use categorical colors at reduced opacity (40–50%) for the fill so underlying grid lines remain visible. The line stroke uses the full categorical color.

## Accessibility

- Do not use stacked area charts with more than 5 series.
- Ensure the fill opacity provides sufficient contrast for the fill boundary.

## ECharts implementation notes

Area fill is applied via `series[].areaStyle`. Stacked areas require `series[].stack: 'total'` on each series.
