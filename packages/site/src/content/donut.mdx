title: Donut & Pie chart
---

## Usage

Donut and pie charts show part-to-whole relationships. Use them sparingly — humans are poor at judging angles. Limit to 5–6 slices maximum; always sort by value descending.

**When to use:**
- Showing proportions that sum to 100%
- 2–6 categories with clearly different proportions

**When not to use:**
- More than 6 categories
- Precise value comparison is needed (use a bar chart)
- Values are similar in size (differences are hard to perceive in a pie)

## Variants

- **Donut** — hollow center; the center can display a total or key value.
- **Pie** — fully filled.

## Color

Use the categorical palette. The first slice starts at the 12 o'clock position and proceeds clockwise in descending value order.

## Accessibility

- Always include a legend identifying each slice.
- Provide exact values in tooltips.
- Do not use pie charts where precise values matter.

## ECharts implementation notes

Donut charts use `radius: ['40%', '70%']`. The center label is rendered via `series[].label` with `position: 'center'`. `createDonutOptions` handles the inner radius and center text automatically.
