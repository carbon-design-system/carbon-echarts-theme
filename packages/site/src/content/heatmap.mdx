## Usage

Heatmaps encode a numeric value as color intensity across a two-dimensional grid. They are effective for showing patterns in large datasets where individual values are less important than the overall distribution.

**When to use:**
- Showing density or concentration across two categorical dimensions
- Identifying patterns, clusters, or anomalies in a matrix
- Calendar heatmaps (activity over days × weeks)

**When not to use:**
- Precise value comparison (color perception is imprecise)
- Sparse matrices with few non-zero cells

## Color

Use a **sequential palette** — a single-hue ramp from low (light) to high (dark). The `@carbon/echarts-theme` sequential palettes are configured for the `visualMap` component automatically.

## Accessibility

- Always include a `visualMap` legend so users can map color to value.
- Provide tooltips with exact values.

## ECharts implementation notes

Heatmaps use `series.type: 'heatmap'` paired with a `visualMap` component. `createHeatmapOptions` configures `visualMap.inRange.color` from the sequential palette automatically.
