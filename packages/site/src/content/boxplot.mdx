## Usage

Boxplots (box-and-whisker plots) display the distribution of a dataset through its quartiles. They are effective for comparing distributions across categories and identifying outliers.

**When to use:**

- Comparing statistical distributions across groups
- Identifying outliers, skewness, or spread
- Summarizing large datasets where individual points would clutter a scatter chart

**When not to use:**

- Fewer than ~20 observations per group (use a dot plot)
- Audiences unfamiliar with statistical charts (add explanatory annotations)

## Color

Use a single categorical color per group, or the categorical palette for multi-group comparison.

## ECharts implementation notes

ECharts `boxplot` series accepts pre-computed `[min, Q1, median, Q3, max]` tuples. `createBoxplotOptions` computes these statistics from raw observations automatically.
